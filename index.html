Selected projects

Neural Signal ML

Decodes a monkey’s hand movement from 152 neurons in motor and premotor cortex, the core problem behind brain-controlled cursors and robot arms. On held-out reaches a GRU decoder scores R² 0.75, against 0.55 for the classic Kalman filter.

Post Training Lab

A 59k-parameter transformer learns a key-and-door maze in two stages: supervised fine-tuning on demos from a planner with a bad habit, then GRPO against a verifiable reward. GRPO lifts held-out success from 71% to 85%; RL from scratch on the same budget reaches 1%.

Post-trainingSFT → GRPOTransformersPyTorch

Reward Hack Zoo

Five tiny post-training environments, each with a naive reward a model can game: length bias, sycophancy, test special-casing, keyword stuffing and self-grading. A 135M-parameter model is trained on each with RL and rejection-sampling SFT, and plots mark the step where the reward it chases pulls away from the score that actually matters. Runs in progress.

AlphaGo Lite

A 9×9 Go engine that taught itself from scratch with the AlphaGo Zero recipe: 7,040 self-play games in about 11 hours on one laptop. Play it and watch which moves it’s weighing.

Sim to Real Drone Bench

Flies one course with a classical controller and RL policies, including a meta-learned one, and measures the sim-to-real gap. Built for a Crazyflie; so far it’s been tested against a deliberately mismatched simulated drone.

Autonomous Driving Sim

A browser self-driving stack, from perception and prediction to planning and control, with a learned speed policy that can only act through a safety check. Tested on 60 drives, with every failure traced to its cause.
