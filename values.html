Values & inspiration

What I try to live by, and the people, books and places behind it.

Values

  1. Do hard things on purpose

    Ironman-distance triathlons, Half Dome, Seattle to DC by bike. The long, uncomfortable stretch is where I learn the most.

  2. Understand, don’t just build

    I want to know why something works, whether it’s a robot’s policy or a neuron’s firing, not only that it does.

  3. Build for everyone

    The best tools let anyone do what they set out to do, wherever they start from.

  4. Lead by going first

    From Eagle Scouts to expeditions and relay teams: take the first step, then make room for others.

  5. Be honest about results

    Report what failed alongside what worked, and trace each failure to its cause.
