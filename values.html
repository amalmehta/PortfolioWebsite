What I try to live by

My values, beliefs, how I want to lead my life, and the people who inspire me.

Values

  1. High agency

    If something matters, I find a way to make it happen instead of waiting for permission.

  2. Ambition

    Picking problems big enough that getting halfway is still worth it.

  3. Deep curiosity

    Following a question until I understand it from the bottom up.

  4. Caring

    Showing up for people, and taking their problems as seriously as my own.

  5. Courage

    Starting before I feel ready, and saying what I think.

Beliefs

What I believe currently

  • Learning rate is a higher quality signal than experience. In a field that changes every year, how fast you learn matters more than what you already know.
  • The biggest things are created by repeated small actions. An Ironman, or Seattle to DC, is just a lot of ordinary days in a row.
  • Simple, but effective beats clever.

Outdated

Things I used to believe, and what changed my mind.

  • Top-tier raw intelligence is needed to be successful. What changed my mind: seeing people who learned fast and kept at it outpace people who started smarter.

Ways I want to lead my life

  • Make things, often, and share them. Every project on this site ships with its code.
  • Tell good stories, and live a few worth telling.
  • Keep pushing my own limits. Five Sierra passes in a day, then frozen waterfalls.
  • Stay physically, mentally and spiritually fit.
  • Understand from first principles, but do from accumulated skill. Like knowing why a network needs an activation function to learn a maze, and then training it well.
  • Seek deep understanding, not just shallow usage. Why I built a tool that teaches a paper equation by equation.
  • Say yes to hard, beautiful things: mountains, long races, the open road.
  • Help others as often as I get the opportunity.

How I lead

  • Lead from the front, but also leave no one behind.
