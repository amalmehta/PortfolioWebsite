Beliefs

What I believe currently

  • Learning rate is a higher quality signal than experience.
  • Autodidactism is the highest quality form of learning.
  • The biggest things are created by repeated small actions.
  • Deep reading and writing are rare skills.
  • Understand from first principles, but do from accumulated skill.
  • Little details are sometimes the most important.
  • Intuition over analysis.
  • Our schools were built for a world that no longer exists.
  • The world is a malleable place.
  • Adventure is the antidote.

My predictions

  • The best robot models will come from teams that build their own hardware.
  • Brain–computer interfaces will be a routine treatment for paralysis within ten years.
  • Haptic feedback will matter first in teleoperation and robot teaching, not consumer devices.
  • Brain–computer interfaces will need touch feedback to feel natural, not just better decoders.
  • The scarce skill won’t be building with AI but checking its work: knowing when a result is wrong.

Let go

  • Top-tier raw intelligence is needed to be successful. What changed my mind: seeing people who learned fast and kept at it outpace people who started smarter.

Ways I want to lead my life

  • Make things, often, and share them.
  • It’s not what you do, but how you do it.
  • Live life with urgency.
  • Experiences over things.
  • Process over outcomes.
  • Be willing to take and give feedback.
  • Allow yourself to be disliked, but don’t do things that make you dislike yourself.
  • Question herd mentality.
  • Do one thing very well at a time.
  • Never sacrifice quality for quantity.
  • Show your work.
  • Lean into your own unique style.
  • Don’t over-optimize.
